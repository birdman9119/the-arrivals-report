Methodology

Where the numbers come from, and what they mean.

Source

All data is the U.S. Department of Transportation's Bureau of Transportation Statistics On-Time Performance database, which covers scheduled passenger flights by U.S. reporting carriers on domestic routes. We use the most recent complete month ({{ month }}).

Definitions

On-time means the flight arrived within 15 minutes of its scheduled arrival time — the DOT's standard definition. Cancelled and diverted flights are counted separately, not as on-time.

Avg. delay is the mean arrival delay in minutes across all arrivals by that airline at that airport (early arrivals count as zero, matching the DOT's "ArrDelayMinutes" measure).

Avg. taxi-in is the mean time in minutes from wheels-on to the gate — the walk off the plane part of your arrival, before the bag claim.

What this is not

There is no public data on baggage claim wait times by airline — no airport or agency publishes it. This site intentionally doesn't pretend otherwise. "Bags lost" rates are published by the DOT only at the national, per-airline level, so they are not shown per airport.

Update schedule

Data is refreshed monthly, within days of each DOT release.
